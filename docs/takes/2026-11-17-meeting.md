# Take 2026-11-17: one-hour meeting at the office

## Conditions

- **Chain:** EasyEffects with awpro-v3 (see [reference chains](../reference-chains.md)).
- **Headset:** mic noise reduction **off**, switched off before the meeting.
- **Office:** at first one coworker nearby, also in a meeting. Toward the end
  a few more people talked not far away. It was not the loudest the office
  gets.
- **Recording:** made with the first version of the script. EasyEffects had
  moved the raw recorder onto its own output, so the raw track was restarted
  on the headset 2 minutes in. That is why raw.flac starts 98.8 s later.

Numbers below come from `scripts/analyze-take.sh`.

## What it showed

### 1. The output clips

- **The raw mic never clips:** its peak is -2.4 dBFS.
- **The processed output clips wherever you talk:** 28,497 samples sit at full
  scale, the true peak is +0.5 dBTP, and in the loudest stretches about a
  third of the voice reaches full scale.
- **It is too loud:** the integrated loudness is -12.8 LUFS.
- **Cause:** +15 dB of makeup gain and no limiter.
- **Visible in the spectrogram:** the processed track has energy above 7 kHz
  that the raw track does not have, which is likely the clipping distortion.

### 2. Little noise reached the raw mic in this take

- **The raw floor is very low:** -84 dBFS (median of the 10 ms RMS), only 1–2
  steps above digital silence.
- **Your voice:** 88.5 s above -40 dBFS, mostly between -40 and -20 dBFS.
- **Sound away from your voice:** 20 stretches of quieter sound at least 1 s
  away from your voice, 11.7 s in total. The chain silenced most of it.

### 3. Background voices still leak

- **Heard on playback:** voices from nearby leak into the processed track.
- **Not measured yet:** the detector above only looks at stretches at least
  1 s away from your voice, so it misses leaks during and right around your
  speech. Measuring those needs a better detector, or speaker recognition.

### 4. Clicks get louder

- Keyboard, mouse and mic-bump clicks pass through DeepFilterNet and the
  expander.
- The makeup gain then raises them by about 15 dB: one click went from
  -60 dBFS raw to about -45 dBFS processed.

### 5. The output is digital silence 95% of the time

- The gate closes fully between words.
- Calls add comfort noise, so it's not a problem in itself.

### 6. How the chain treats each raw level

- **Below about -72 dBFS:** fully silenced.
- **Between -69 and -48 dBFS:** passes partly, and is raised by 12–18 dB on the
  way.
- **Your voice:** comes out about 14–16 dB louder, compressed at the top.

### 7. Latency

The processed track lags the raw one by about 75 ms. DeepFilterNet accounts
for 20 ms of that (the low-latency model: 10 ms of model delay and 10 ms of
plugin buffering, plus 10 ms steps when its worker falls behind), and
EasyEffects and PipeWire buffering for the rest.

### 8. The voice cuts, already in the raw mic (5:20–6:00)

- **Heard:** in the 2026-11-19 listening test, Romain heard the voice cut a
  lot around 5:20–6:00, in the raw track too. That matches what people
  said in the call at that moment.
- **So it happens before any processing:** in the headset or its wireless
  link, not in EasyEffects or fabrial.
- **What the numbers show:** the speech is more broken up there. In
  minutes 4 and 5 of the raw track, 36–41 % of the bursts of sound
  (above -50 dBFS) last 80 ms or less, against 22–30 % in minutes 42–50,
  with more bursts per second of voice (6.9 in minute 5, 3.4–4.6 in minutes
  44–46).
- **What it isn't:** there are no holes of digital silence inside words,
  and sound fades out over 12–25 ms, as speech does, not instantly. The USB
  dongle didn't drop: the kernel logged nothing during the meeting.
- **Not known:** whether the headset's own noise reduction had switched
  itself back on, which it does whenever the headset restarts, without the
  USB dongle noticing. This take didn't record the headset's state.
  `scripts/record-take.sh` now logs it every 10 s.

## Next steps this take suggests

- **Add a limiter and lower the makeup gain:** about +9–10 dB, for -18 to -16 LUFS.
- **Record a take with the headset's noise reduction on.** Your impression
  is that it clips your voice more easily when people talk around you; one
  take with it on lets us measure that.
- **Record a take in a loud moment.**
- **Measure leaks during speech, not only between words.**
